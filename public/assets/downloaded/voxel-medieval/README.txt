Voxel Medieval Village Kit Free
===============================
10 voxel props: a thatched cottage, a rigged peasant and hen, a grass tile and a
dirt path tile, a rail fence, a barrel, a crate, an oak and a sword.
Made and licensed by heyheythere.

GLB and FBX with their animations, OBJ (static); the .blend source, colour
atlas, PNG icons and props.json are included. This folder is a Godot 4.3+
project with a gallery. Copy addons/voxel_medieval_kit/ into your project; the
full pack replaces it in the same folder.

Metre scale. In Blender, +Z is up and everything faces -Y; in Godot, +Y is up and
it faces +Z. The tiles are 4 x 4 m, their top at 0 m. The cottage's door opens
(its "open" clip). The peasant idles, walks, attacks, flinches and dies; the hen
idles, walks, runs, eats, lies down, sleeps, stands up, attacks, flinches and
dies, its walk and run in place. The fence is 2 m long along X; the sword lies
flat along X, its blade to +X.
